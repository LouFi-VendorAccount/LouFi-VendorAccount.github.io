# Business FAQs

## Do I pay you? 
Anyone can connect to LouFi for free.

LouFi members pay us monthly for our full range of services.

## What does it cost? 
It depends! A site's square footage, WiFi coverage needs, and WiFi interference all affect the cost.

That said, here are some ranges:

smaller spaces, like coffee shops, can range from $200 to $500,
larger spaces, like co-working spaces, can range from $500 to $1000.

# Technical FAQs

## Is it secure?
Yes! Clients are on an isolated VLAN, and are isolated from each other.

## How fast is fast? 
4K streaming fast! Every client gets 25 Mbps download bandwidth. The overall shared bandwidth depends on the partner.

## What bands are supported? 
It depends on a site's coverage needs.

In low-density deployments, LouFi uses the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz bands.
In high-density deployments, LouFi uses the 5 GHz and 6 GHz bands.
